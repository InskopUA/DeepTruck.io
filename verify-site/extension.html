DeepTruck Verify

Chrome extension

Verify on Central Dispatch.

Download the extension and add it to Chrome in a few steps.

Download Extension

  1. Unzip the download

    Keep the extracted DeepTruck Verify folder on your computer.

  2. Open Chrome extensions

    Enter chrome://extensions in Chrome’s address bar, then turn on Developer mode.

  3. Add DeepTruck to Chrome

    Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.

  4. Sign in and start verifying

    Open DeepTruck from Chrome’s toolbar and sign in. On a Central Dispatch carrier profile, click Verify carrier.

FAQ

What shipper teams usually ask before adding verification to their load workflow.

common questions
What does DeepTruck Verify check?

It verifies the carrier email, confirms the listed phone with SMS, and collects driver license, W-9, and certificate of insurance documents before load details are shared.

How often are vehicles stolen in the U.S.?

Vehicle theft is still a large market risk: NICB reported 1,020,729 stolen vehicles in 2023, 850,708 in 2024, and 659,880 in 2025. Even after the decline, that is hundreds of thousands of stolen vehicles per year, which is why pre-load identity checks matter before releasing inventory.

NICB 2024 theft report
Why not trust Central Dispatch alone?

Central Dispatch is the workflow shipper teams already use, but a marketplace profile does not prove the person replying today is the legitimate carrier or driver. Central Dispatch warns users about phishing and credential harvesting, and the FBI has investigated schemes involving fake or compromised Central Dispatch accounts. DeepTruck adds a separate verification layer before load details are released.

Central Dispatch security guidance
Does it replace FMCSA or Central Dispatch?

No. It works on top of the existing carrier profile and creates a shipper-side verification record for the specific carrier request.

Can my team see verification history?

Yes. The shipper console keeps request history, status, timestamps, and uploaded documents for review.

What happens after the monthly limit?

Extra verification requests are billed at $0.50 per request after the included monthly volume.
